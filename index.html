How we build

Small team, careful choices.

Works on a weak connection

Velto and Arrow Paint run without the internet. NeetQuest keeps practice and mock tests working on patchy mobile data.

No needless sign-ups

Velto and Arrow Paint never ask you to make an account. NeetQuest uses one to save and sync your progress.

Privacy in plain words

Every app has a short privacy policy that says what is collected and why. Velto never uploads your files.
