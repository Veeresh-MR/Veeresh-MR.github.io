Features

Everything in Velto

Velto is a free PDF reader, PDF editor and PDF scanner for Android. Read PDFs aloud with word-by-word highlighting, scan paper to PDF, sign, merge, compress and convert, and open documents, spreadsheets and presentations. Every PDF tool works offline, there is no account, and your files never leave your phone.

PDF reader and viewer

  • Fast viewer with sharp text at every zoom level
  • Read aloud: text to speech that highlights each word as it is spoken
  • Night reading, bookmarks, search inside a PDF, resume where you stopped
  • Opens PDFs shared from chat and email apps

Scan to PDF

  • Scan documents, notes and receipts with your camera
  • Automatic edge detection, crop and straighten
  • OCR makes scans searchable, so you can copy the text

PDF editor

  • Highlight, draw and annotate
  • Add text and sticky notes anywhere on a page
  • Draw your signature once and reuse it on any document
  • Watermark, page numbers and crop margins

Every PDF tool

  • Merge, split, extract and delete pages
  • Reorder and rotate pages by dragging
  • Compress PDFs, image to PDF, PDF to image and PDF to text
  • Remove the password from a PDF you can already open

Docs, Sheets and Slides

  • Documents (.docx, .doc, .rtf, .txt) open on their real pages and can be edited
  • Spreadsheets (.xlsx, .xls, .csv) with formulas, sorting and several sheets
  • Presentations (.pptx, .ppt) slide by slide
  • Save any of them as a PDF in one tap

Private and secure

  • Every PDF tool runs on your phone and works offline
  • No account and no sign-in; your files are never uploaded
  • Lock the app, or a single PDF, with a PIN or fingerprint

Privacy in plain words

Velto has no server and no upload path. Documents are opened, edited and saved only on your phone. One small banner ad sits at the foot of the reading screen.

Questions

Good to know

Is there a free PDF reader for Android that works offline?

Yes. Velto is a free Android PDF reader, editor and scanner. Every PDF tool runs on the phone and works offline, with no account or sign-in.

How do I make my phone read a PDF out loud?

Open the PDF in Velto and tap Listen. It reads the document aloud using your phone’s own voice and highlights each word as it is spoken.

How can I merge PDF files on Android for free?

In Velto, open Tools, choose Merge, pick the PDFs in order and save. The merge happens on your phone, so nothing is uploaded.

Does Velto upload my PDFs anywhere?

No. Velto has no server and no upload path. Documents are opened, edited and saved only on your phone.
