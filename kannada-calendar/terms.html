4. Panchanga, festivals and holidays

  • Tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, sunrise, sunset, moonrise, Rahu Kala and other timings are calculated on your phone from astronomical formulas (the Drik method with the Lahiri ayanamsa) for the place you choose. Timings for a nearby place, or from another method, can differ by a few minutes.
  • Festival dates follow the Smarta tradition. Some families, mathas and temples follow other traditions, and a festival may then fall a day apart.
  • Karnataka government holidays follow the pattern of the government’s published list. Holidays based on moon sighting are marked as tentative. Always check the official notification for holidays that matter to you.
  • For muhurtas, weddings, ceremonies and other important events, please also consult your family priest or astrologer.
