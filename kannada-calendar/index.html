Features

Everything in Kannada Calendar

Kannada Calendar brings the printed panchanga of every Karnataka home to your phone: tithi, nakshatra, Rahu Kala and sunrise for any of 31,000+ towns and villages, festivals and government holidays, Rashi readings and a free Janma Kundli in Kannada. It works offline, and your birth details never leave your phone.

Printed-panchanga calendar

  • Weekday blocks with Rahu Kala and Gulika times, masa, paksha and tithi on every date
  • Festival days in yellow, Sundays and holidays in dark red, Hunnime and Amavasye marked with the moon
  • A “Special days” box with the month’s festivals and vrata dates
  • A Raja Ravi Varma painting for every month, and a new devotional painting each time you open the app

Daily Panchanga

  • Tithi, nakshatra, yoga and karana with exact ending times
  • Sunrise, sunset, moonrise and moonset for your own town or village
  • Rahu Kala, Yamaganda, Gulika, Durmuhurta, Brahma and Abhijit Muhurta
  • The Moon as it looks today, with how much of it is lit

Festivals and holidays

  • Ugadi, Varamahalakshmi, Gowri–Ganesha, Dasara, Deepavali, Sankranti and more
  • Karnataka government holidays and second and fourth Saturdays
  • Ekadashi, Sankashti with moonrise time, Hunnime, Amavasye and Pradosha

Rashi and free Kundli

  • Today’s and this month’s reading for your rashi, with Chandrashtama dates and Sade Sati status
  • Free Janma Kundli PDF: Rasi and Navamsa charts, planets, houses, dasha and bhuktis
  • Your nature, the yogas in your chart and what your current dasha means
  • Readings for education, career, money, love, marriage, health, family and travel

Notifications you choose

  • A morning Panchanga with the day’s tithi, nakshatra and Rahu Kala
  • Optional alerts before Rahu Kala and the day’s best time
  • Optional reminders the evening before Hunnime, Amavasye, Ekadashi and festivals
  • Home-screen widgets with today’s date and Panchanga

Stotras, notes and sharing

  • Vishnu Sahasranama, Hanuman Chalisa, Suprabhatam, Aditya Hridayam and more, in Kannada script
  • Save birthdays, poojas and reminders on any date
  • Share the day’s Panchanga on WhatsApp as a picture

Privacy in plain words

All calculations happen on your phone. Your birth details and notes are never uploaded. A short ad from Google AdMob is shown before a Kundli is created.

Questions

Good to know

Does Kannada Calendar work without the internet?

Yes. The calendar, Panchanga, festivals, Rashi readings and Kundli are all calculated on your phone, so they work offline. Only the short ad before a Kundli needs a connection.

Are the timings correct for my village?

Sunrise, Rahu Kala and every other timing is calculated for the place you choose, from over 31,000 towns and villages in Karnataka.

Which tradition do the festival dates follow?

Festival dates follow the Smarta tradition. Some families, mathas and temples follow other traditions, so a festival may fall a day apart. Always confirm government holidays with the official notification.

Is my birth information uploaded anywhere?

No. The birth details you enter for a Kundli stay on your phone and are used only to calculate it there.
