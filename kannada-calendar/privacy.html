Kannada Calendar Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026 · Kannada Calendar 2027 Panchang for Android, by The Quest Studio

Kannada Calendar is a free Kannada calendar and Panchanga app for Android. It calculates the Panchanga, festivals, Rashi readings and your Janma Kundli on your own phone, with no account and no sign-in. This policy explains, in plain words, what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • There is no account. We never ask for your email address or phone number.
  • The birth details you enter for a Kundli or Rashi (name, date, time and place of birth) are used on your phone to calculate it and are saved only on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your notes, chosen place and settings are saved only on your phone.
  • The app is free because it shows a short ad from Google AdMob before a Kundli is created. Google’s ad software collects some technical data to show it, explained under Advertising.
  • We do not use analytics, and we do not sell your personal data.

What we do not collect

  • Your email address, phone number or any other contact detail.
  • Your contacts, photos, files, messages or call history.
  • Your GPS location. You choose your town or village from a list built into the app; the app has no location permission. Google’s ad software may estimate a rough location from your IP address, as described below.
  • Camera or microphone input. The app has no access to either.
  • Payment details. The app has no purchases.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps the following in its own private storage on your device. It is not sent to us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data:

  • Your saved birth details, if you created a Kundli or found your Rashi, so you do not have to type them again.
  • Your chosen Rashi, place, language, theme and calendar style.
  • Notes you add to dates, such as birthdays and poojas.
  • Which notifications you turned on and at what time.
  • The day’s Panchanga prepared for the home-screen widget.

All Panchanga, festival, Rashi and Kundli calculations happen on your phone, and the list of places is built into the app, so none of this needs the internet. If Android backup is switched on, Android may include this app data in your own Google account backup. That backup is managed by Google and Android; we cannot see it.

Kundli PDF and sharing

A Kundli PDF is created on your phone. When you tap Download it is saved to your phone’s Downloads folder; when you tap Share, or share the day’s Panchanga picture, it goes only to the app and people you choose. We do not receive a copy.

Advertising

Before a Kundli is created, the app offers a short reward ad served by Google AdMob; the Kundli opens after the ad. Ads are the only reason the app uses the internet. To show the ad, measure it and prevent fraud, Google’s ad software in the app automatically collects the following and shares it with Google and, when Google’s ad auction uses one, with the ad partner whose ad is shown:

  • Your device’s advertising ID and app set ID: resettable identifiers for the device, not for you by name.
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate a general location, roughly your city.
  • Interactions with ads, such as ad views and taps.
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance.

Your birth details, notes and Kundli are never given to the ad software. How Google uses ad data is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your ad choices

  • Where the law requires it, including the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google’s consent form before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in More → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your phone’s settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads; the menu varies by phone).

Notifications

Notifications are off until you allow them. The morning Panchanga and any reminders you turn on (Rahu Kala, best time, Hunnime, Amavasye, Ekadashi, Sankashti, Pradosha and festivals) are calculated and scheduled on your phone itself. No personal data is sent to create them. Turn them off in More → Notifications or in your phone’s notification settings.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only for ads. The calendar, Panchanga and Kundli work without a connection.
  • Notifications: only for the notifications you turn on.
  • Run at startup: so notifications you turned on still arrive after your phone restarts.
  • Advertising ID: added by Google’s ad software, as described under Advertising.

Who we share data with

Only Google, as the provider of AdMob, and the ad partners described under Advertising. We do not sell personal data and do not share it with data brokers. If we ever add another service that receives data, we will update this policy first. Google’s practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it in the app, uninstall the app or clear its data. Data received by Google’s ad services is kept under Google’s own retention rules.

Security

Ad requests to Google are encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS). Your saved details live in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Children

Kannada Calendar is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the GDPR (Europe and the UK) and US state privacy laws may give you the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is used. The app has no accounts and keeps your details only on your phone, so you are in control. You can:

  • change or replace your saved birth details in the Kundli form at any time;
  • delete notes inside the app;
  • change your ad consent in More → Privacy options, where shown;
  • reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings;
  • uninstall the app, or clear its data in your phone’s settings, to remove everything it stored.

For any other request, email us at the address below. We reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Kannada Calendar collects anything it does not collect today, we will update this page, and the app’s Data safety information on Google Play, before that version is released. The date at the top shows the latest change.
