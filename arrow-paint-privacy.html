Arrow Paint Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026 · Arrow Paint for Android, by The Quest Studio

Arrow Paint is a free puzzle game for Android. Tap the arrows in the right order, launch them off the board and use their paint to color a pixel-art picture every level. It plays offline, has no account and no sign-in. This policy explains, in plain words, what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • There is no account. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress, coins, pictures and settings are saved on your phone.
  • The game is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob. Google’s ad software collects some technical data to show them, explained under Advertising.
  • If the game crashes, a crash report is sent to us through Google Firebase so we can fix the problem.
  • Play stats are off unless you choose to turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any other contact detail.
  • Your contacts, photos, files, messages or call history.
  • Your precise (GPS) location. Google’s software may estimate a rough location from your IP address, as described below.
  • Camera or microphone input. The game has no access to either.
  • Payment details. Arrow Paint has no real-money purchases; coins are earned by playing.

What stays on your phone

The game keeps the following in its own private storage on your device. It is not sent to us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data:

  • Your level, coins, stars, boosters and the finished pictures in your album.
  • Your Endless mode best score.
  • Your settings: music, sound, vibration, reminders and play stats.
  • Small notes so the game does not repeat itself, such as which tips you have already seen and whether you have answered the rating card.

If Android backup is switched on, Android may include this game data in your own Google account backup, so your progress can return on a new phone. That backup is managed by Google and Android; we cannot see it.

Advertising

Arrow Paint is supported by ads supplied by Google AdMob: banner ads, full-screen ads at natural breaks, and optional reward videos you can choose to watch for an in-game bonus. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads software in the game automatically collects the following and shares it with Google:

  • Your device’s advertising ID and app set ID: resettable identifiers for the device, not for you by name.
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate a general location, roughly your city.
  • Interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, ad views, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use.

How Google uses this data is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your ad choices

  • Where the law requires it, including the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google’s consent form before personalized ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your phone’s settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads; the menu varies by phone).

Crash reports

If the game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (a Google service) sends us a crash report. It contains technical details of the error and the state of the game at the time, device details such as the phone model and Android version, and a random installation ID used to count how many players a problem affects. It contains no name or contact details. We use crash reports only to find and fix bugs.

Play stats (optional, off by default)

The game may ask you once whether you would like to share play stats. Only if you say yes, Google Analytics for Firebase receives game events such as levels started, won or failed, Endless mode runs, how in-game coins and boosters are used, reward videos watched, and whether the rating card was shown or tapped. Google Analytics also collects an app-instance ID, the advertising ID, a coarse location derived from a masked IP address, and screen views and sessions. These stats are not linked to your name or contact details. We use them only to see which levels feel too hard or too easy and to make the game better.

You can turn play stats off at any time in Settings → Share play stats. Collection then stops, and the analytics data stored on your phone is reset.

Game settings updates

So we can adjust game settings without releasing an app update, the game checks Firebase Remote Config. This request includes basic technical information: country and language, time zone, Android and app version, and a random Firebase installation ID which, according to Google, does not uniquely identify a user or a physical device.

Notifications

  • Reminders are off until you say yes. They are scheduled on your phone itself, arrive at most once a day, and stop on their own if you have not played for a week. No personal data is sent to create them. Turn them off in Settings → Reminders or in your phone’s notification settings.
  • We may occasionally send news, such as new pictures, through Firebase Cloud Messaging. Delivery uses a random installation token and basic technical information such as the app version. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone’s settings.

Rating the game

If you tap Rate on Google Play, the game opens Arrow Paint’s page on Google Play. Any rating or review you post there is handled by Google Play under Google’s own terms.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for ads, crash reports, optional play stats and game settings. The puzzles themselves work without a connection.
  • Notifications: only for reminders you turn on, and occasional news.
  • Run at startup: so reminders you turned on still arrive after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID: added by Google’s ad software, as described under Advertising.

Who we share data with

Only Google, as the provider of AdMob and Firebase, as described above. We do not sell personal data and do not share it with data brokers. If we ever add another advertising partner, we will update this policy first. Google’s practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy information.

How long data is kept

Game data on your phone stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data received by Google’s services is kept only for limited periods under Google’s retention rules and our Firebase settings, and is then deleted automatically.

Security

Everything the game sends to Google is encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS). Game data on your phone lives in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Children

Arrow Paint is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the GDPR (Europe and the UK), India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act and US state privacy laws may give you the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is used. Arrow Paint has no accounts, so we do not hold your name or contact details, and data collected by Google’s services is linked only to random identifiers. You can:

  • turn play stats off in Settings;
  • change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy options, where shown;
  • reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings;
  • uninstall the game to remove everything it stored on your phone.

For any other request, email us at the address below. We reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Arrow Paint collects anything it does not collect today, we will update this page, and the game’s Data safety information on Google Play, before that version is released. The date at the top shows the latest change.
