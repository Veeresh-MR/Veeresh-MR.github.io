NeetQuest

A serious NEET question bank in your pocket, with mock tests that show exactly where your marks are leaking.

  • Free with ads
  • Optional Premium
  • Works on weak data
  • Made in India
Privacy policy Terms

Features

Everything in NeetQuest

NeetQuest puts 2.35 lakh+ practice questions, 27 years of solved previous papers and full 720-mark mock tests in your pocket, and shows you, chapter by chapter, where your marks are leaking. Practice keeps working on patchy mobile data.

What is inside

  • 2.35 lakh+ practice questions across Physics, Chemistry, Botany and Zoology
  • 27 years of previous year papers, 2000 to 2026, every question solved
  • Full 720-mark mock tests with real exam timing and +4/−1 marking
  • 31,000+ flashcards, 900+ mnemonics and 83 chapters

Fast, even on weak data

  • Questions open instantly
  • Practice, past papers and mock tests keep working on patchy mobile data
  • A connection is needed only to sign in and sync your progress

Practice your way

  • Pick a chapter and choose 10 questions, 30, or everything left in it
  • Short sessions before class or a long grind at night

Know where you stand

  • An estimated All India Rank after every mock, from published score-to-rank data
  • A chapter-wise breakdown of where you lose marks
  • Export a full PDF report to keep or show a teacher

Build the habit

  • Daily missions, streaks, XP, levels and badges
  • A leaderboard against other aspirants

Free and Premium

  • The question bank, previous papers, mock tests and flashcards are free, supported by ads
  • Premium removes ads and adds Deep Analytics
  • Subscriptions renew through Google Play and can be cancelled any time

Privacy in plain words

NeetQuest uses an account only to save and sync your progress. It is an independent study app, not affiliated with NTA, NCERT or any examination body.
