Questions

Is there a free PDF reader for Android that works offline?

Yes. Velto is a free Android PDF reader, editor and scanner. Every PDF tool runs on the phone and works offline, with no account or sign-in.

How do I make my phone read a PDF out loud?

Open the PDF in Velto and tap Listen. It reads the document aloud using your phone's own voice and highlights each word as it is spoken. You can also select one paragraph and tap Read to hear only that part.

How can I merge PDF files on Android for free?

In Velto, open Tools, choose Merge, pick the PDFs in the order you want and save. The merge happens on your phone, so nothing is uploaded.

How do I scan a document to PDF on Android?

In Velto, tap New and choose Scan paper. The scanner finds the page edges, straightens and crops the photo, and can add a text layer so the scanned PDF is searchable.

Can I sign a PDF on my phone for free?

Yes. Open the PDF in Velto, tap the pencil, choose Signature, draw it once and place it on the page. It is saved for reuse on any other document.

How do I reduce the size of a PDF on Android?

Use Compress in Velto's Tools. It saves a smaller copy on your device and leaves the original untouched.

Does Velto upload my PDFs anywhere?

No. Velto has no server and no upload path. Documents are opened, edited and saved only on your phone.

Is Velto really free?

Yes. Every feature is free. Velto shows one small banner ad at the bottom of the reading screen, never covering the document.
