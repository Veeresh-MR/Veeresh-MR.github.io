Features

Everything in Arrow Paint

Every arrow carries paint. Tap it, watch it fly off the board and splash its colour into a pixel-art picture. Find the right order, clear the board and bring a new picture to life every level.

Tap, launch, paint

  • Tap an arrow and it flies off in the direction it points
  • Each arrow paints a part of the picture
  • Plan your moves: a blocked arrow costs a heart
  • Clear the board and earn 3 stars

Brain teaser meets relaxing art

  • Hundreds of levels, from calm and easy to super hard
  • Surprise twists with Mystery, Ice and Bomb arrows
  • Chain fast moves into satisfying combos
  • Hints and extra Slots when you get stuck

Boss levels and Daily Challenge

  • Boss levels with giant pictures to paint
  • A fresh Daily Challenge every day
  • A special Trophy Gold skin each month

Collect a world of pixel art

  • Themed albums with cute animals, yummy treats and more
  • Complete albums for big coin rewards
  • Replay favourite levels for a perfect clear

Make it yours

  • Pixel Studio: draw your own pixel art and turn it into a puzzle
  • Endless mode: board after board, beat your high score
  • Arrow skins, trails, tile patterns, scenes and sound packs

Play offline

  • Play anywhere: on the train, on a flight or in bed
  • Quick levels for any spare minute
  • Calm music, satisfying sounds and gentle vibration

Privacy in plain words

There is no account. Your progress is saved on your phone. The game is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob.
