Velto — Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Velto is a PDF reader and editor that works entirely on your phone. It has no account to create, no server to sign in to, and no analytics of its own. It shows one advert, at the foot of the reading screen, and that is the only part of the app that involves anyone else. This policy explains, in plain terms, what the app touches and what it does not.

The short version

Your documents are opened, read and edited on your device and are never uploaded anywhere. Velto itself collects no personal data.

There is no account and no login.

The app shows one advert, supplied by Google AdMob, at the foot of the reading screen. Google’s advertising software collects some technical data to show it — explained in full under Advertising below. It never receives anything about your documents.

What we do not collect

We do not collect any of the following, at all:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any other contact detail.
  • The contents, names, locations or page counts of your PDFs.
  • Photographs or scans you make with the app.
  • Your precise location. (The advertising software estimates a rough location from your IP address — see Advertising.)
  • Your contacts, calendar, messages or call history.
  • Analytics or usage statistics of our own. Velto contains no analytics software; the only data that leaves your phone is what the advertising software collects, described under Advertising.
  • Anything at all about your documents, for advertising or for any other purpose. The advert at the foot of the reader is not targeted on what you are reading, because nothing about what you are reading ever leaves the phone.

What stays on your phone

Velto saves a small amount of information so that it is useful the second time you open it. All of it lives in the app’s own private storage on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • The list of documents you recently opened and the page you stopped on.
  • Documents you have starred, and bookmarks you have added inside them.
  • Highlights, drawings, notes, typed text and your saved signature.
  • Your display and reading preferences.
  • If you set up the app lock, your PIN is stored only as a salted cryptographic hash. The PIN itself is never written down, and the hash cannot be turned back into it.
  • Files that other apps hand to Velto to open are copied into a temporary folder so they can be read, and are cleared automatically after a few days.

Permissions, and exactly why each one exists

  • All files access — so Velto can find and list the PDFs already on your phone and show them as a library. It is requested only when you open the Files screen and ask for it, it is used to read and list documents, and the app falls back to the system file picker if you refuse. Velto never writes outside its own folders and the folder you choose to save into.
  • Biometrics — only to unlock the app, or an individual PDF, if you have switched the app lock on. Fingerprint and face data stay inside your phone’s secure hardware; the app never sees them.
  • Camera — Velto does not hold the camera permission. When you use the Scan tool, Google Play services opens its own scanner screen and hands the finished pages back to the app. The photographs stay on your device.
  • Internet — used for one thing only: the first time you use the Scan tool, Google Play services downloads the on-device scanning model. No document, page, filename or any other content of yours is ever transmitted.

Text recognition and scanning

Reading the text out of a scan, and detecting the edges of a page, are done by Google’s ML Kit running on your device. The images are processed locally and are not uploaded. The first use may download the recognition model from Google Play services; this transfers the model to your phone and sends none of your content in return.

Reading aloud

Read aloud uses the text-to-speech voice already installed on your phone. Whether that voice works offline depends on the voice you have installed, which is a setting of your device rather than of Velto. The text of your document is handed to that engine on the device, in the same way any app passes text to it.

Links inside documents

A PDF can contain a web link. Velto always shows you the full address and asks before opening anything, and will only open ordinary web, email and telephone links. Once you choose to open one, it is handled by another app on your phone and this policy no longer applies to it.

Sharing and saving

When you tap Share, Android’s own share sheet appears and you choose where the file goes. When you save, the file is written to a folder on your own phone. Velto has no cloud storage and no upload path of any kind.

Advertising

One banner advert is shown along the bottom of the reading screen. It is supplied by Google AdMob, and it is the only feature in Velto that involves a company other than you and your phone.

To show the advert, the Google Mobile Ads software inside Velto automatically collects the following and shares it with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. It is sent encrypted:

  • Your device’s advertising ID and app set ID — resettable numbers that identify the device, not you.
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate a rough location (roughly your city). Velto never asks for or uses GPS.
  • Your interactions with the advert, such as whether it was shown or tapped, and basic app launch events.
  • Diagnostic information about how the app and the advertising software are performing.

Your control over advertising

None of this includes your documents, their names or their contents. What Google does with the data above is covered by Google’s own privacy policy, at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • If you live somewhere that requires consent, Velto asks before any advert is requested, and shows nothing if you decline.
  • You can reset or delete the advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Android also offers an “opt out of ads personalisation” setting, which Velto and Google both honour.
  • No advert is ever placed over a page, and none appears while you are marking up a document or listening to one.

Children

Velto is a general-purpose document tool and is not directed at children under 18.

Your control over your data

Everything Velto keeps is on your device. You can delete any individual document, bookmark, highlight or saved signature inside the app, and removing the app removes everything else with it. Because nothing is collected, there is no copy anywhere for us to delete on your behalf.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app collects anything it does not collect today, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app’s Data safety information on Google Play will be updated with it at the same time.
